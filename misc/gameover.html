Chapter 1

The screen cuts to black. A droning sound plays.

If no Chapter 1 completion file is present, the window is titled "THE DEATH".

First time

Voice IT APPEARS YOU HAVE REACHED   AN END. Voice WILL YOU TRY AGAIN?

Subsequent times

Voice WILL YOU PERSIST?

The droning cuts to silence.

First time only

Voice THEN, THE FUTURE IS IN YOUR HANDS.

The screen fades to white, then cuts back to the most recent autosave.

If no Chapter 1 completion file exists, the window title changes to "THE DARK".

Voice THEN THE WORLD WAS COVERED IN DARKNESS.

The text disappears, leaving the screen a pitch black. Darkness Falls begins playing; once the song plays to completion, the game ends (PC) / returns to the title screen or Chapter Select screen (console).

Chapter 2 onwards

Initial

The screen cuts to black, and large, elaborately-bordered text reading GAMEOVER appears at the top. Faint Courage plays. If there are other party members besides Kris, additional dialogue plays. Otherwise, the screen proceeds to display the choice.

If Susie or Ralsei are in the party

The two variant dialogues below are randomly chosen from. A character's dialogue is not displayed if they are not in the party.

Susie's dialogue

Susie Come on, that all you got!? Susie Kris, get up...!

Ralsei's dialogue

Ralsei This is not your fate...! Ralsei Please, don't give up!

In both cases, dialogue then proceeds to the choice.

Choice

Options appear towards the bottom of the screen:

The screen fades to white, then cuts back to the most recent autosave.

The GAMEOVER text disappears.

Voice THEN THE WORLD WAS COVERED IN DARKNESS.

This text also disappears, leaving the screen a pitch black. Darkness Falls begins playing; once the song plays to completion, the game ends (PC) / returns to the title screen or Chapter Select screen (console).

Battle against the Roaring Knight

Chapter 3's exception to the usual Game Over process.

Before completing Chapter 3 at least once, a Game Over against the Roaring Knight does not result in the usual Game Over screen, instead proceeding to the cutscene immediately after.

Once a Chapter 3 completion file has been obtained, successive Game Overs against the Knight instead use the messages in this section.

First loss

The screen cuts to black. Rather than shattering, the SOUL moves towards the top of the screen. Droning begins to sound as text appears underneath:

Voice    VERY INTERESTING. Voice YOUR LOSS HERE     IS ALL BUT GUARANTEED. Voice    AND YET YOU PERSIST... Voice IF YOU ARE SO DETERMINED TO TRY ONCE MORE Voice       THEN SHALL WE HASTEN?

Dialogue proceeds to the choice.

Second loss

The screen cuts to black. Rather than shattering, the SOUL moves towards the top of the screen. Droning begins to sound as text appears underneath, varying depending on the party's equipment.

Standard

Voice AND SO, YOU MEET WITH THE SAME FATE. Voice SHALL YOU TRY ONCE MORE?

If the Shadow Mantle is not equipped by any party member

Voice    VERY INTERESTING. Voice YOU ARE MISSING SOMETHING IMPORTANT. Voice YOU WON'T WIN LIKE THIS. Voice STILL... WILL YOU PERSIST?

Dialogue proceeds to the choice.

If the Knight was brought to 80% HP or lower

Plays only the first time this condition is met. Overrides all other dialogues.

The screen cuts to black. Rather than shattering, the SOUL moves towards the top of the screen. Droning begins to sound as text appears underneath:

Voice INCREDIBLE. Voice I FELT IT THERE   SHINING. Voice YOUR POWER. Voice A LITTLE FURTHER.

Subsequent losses

The screen cuts to black. Rather than shattering, the SOUL moves towards the top of the screen. Droning begins to sound as the choice appears without additional commentary.

Choice

After a pause, the following choices appear:

The droning cuts off and the screen fades to white, then back in to the TV World's Dark Fountain with the Knight and battle-ready party. After the first loss only, the Knight repeats its roar. The Knight summons its sword and once again draws the party into an encounter.

The screen fades to black, then back in on the encounter but with the battle UI gone. All party members are DOWNed or SWOONed while the Knight still floats to the right. The scene progresses to its end.
