--- title: Weapons description: >

Names, descriptions, and reactions to weapons equippable by the party members.

Characters that can equip a given weapon are listed at the top of the corresponding section. Generally, certain weapon types can only be equipped by certain characters (swords for Kris, axes for Susie, scarves for Ralsei, rings for Noelle). There are exceptions to this rule, however, so make sure to check!
