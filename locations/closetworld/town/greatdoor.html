Great Door

(It seems to be locked.) (It may never open again.)

Bloxer

Chapter 2

Present after the Card Kingdom Darkners have been retrieved.

Before going to Cyber World

Instead of one torso block and one legs block, Bloxer has three leg blocks, but looks pleased.

Bloxer ... I like being like this. Bloxer Don't tell anybody.

After returning from Cyber World, if Tasque Manager was not recruited

The stray animals Tasque, Maus, and Poppup are wandering around nearby if recruited.

First time, if any stray animals are present
Bloxer These animals are stray...? Bloxer No, they are practicing a special loneliness style. Bloxer ... Just like me. Bloxer Lone wolves have to stick together.

A wolf-like howl sounds.

Subsequent times (stray animals present) / All times (no stray animals present)
Bloxer Train at the party dojo? Ha... Bloxer We will make our own dojo... for introverts.

After returning from Cyber World, if Tasque Manager was recruited

Bloxer now has 4 leg blocks instead of 3.

First time
Bloxer ... I've been training. Bloxer ... Don't ask what I've been training, though.
Subsequent times
Bloxer ... are you impressed...?

Chapter 4

Bloxer has been reduced to a head block alone and looks distressed.

First time

Bloxer ... I went too far with my training. Bloxer ... My legs have achieved a kind of ultra-instinct... Bloxer ... Where they can lead their own independent life without me.

Subsequent times

Bloxer ... I would not mind. Bloxer ... If only my legs were also introverted.
