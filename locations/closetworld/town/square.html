SAVE point

Chapter 1

The location is listed as "Castle Town".

Before Ralsei joins the party

In front of you, a castle looms beneath the empty town. A black geyser emerges from it, piercing endlessly into the sky. The power of this place shines within you.

After Ralsei joins the party

Ralsei, the lonely prince, is now your ally. The power of fluffy boys shines within you.

Chapter 2

Before retrieving the Card Kingdom Darkners

The location is listed as "Castle Town".

You look upon the castle you first saw yesterday... You are filled with the power of immediate nostalgia.

After retrieving the Card Kingdom Darkners, before visiting Cyber World

From this point onwards, the location is listed as "My Castle Town". This narration only plays upon the first interaction after entering this room.

The castle town has transformed from the power of friendship. You are filled with the power of friendship-based architecture.

After returning from the Cyber World

May be overridden by certain Weird Route narration.

First time
(You touched the light...) (You thought about how you can't go back to the Cyber World anymore.) (You considered this carefully!)
Subsequent times
After a long day, the town has grown once again. You are filled with a certain power...

After scaring Noelle in the hospital

Exclusive to the Weird Route; plays if, after returning from Cyber World, you scared Noelle in the hospital by stepping towards her (moving right) thrice. Overrides the standard post-Cyber World narration, including the warning about not being able to return to Cyber World.

After a long day, you have returned to the castle town. But, you still feel the power in your hands...

Chapter 4

This narration does not play on the Weird Route.

If you didn't recruit everyone in previous chapters

Missing any of the Darkners from Card Kingdom, Cyber World, or TV World.

(Once again you find yourself in a familiar town.) (You are filled with the power of conspicuously normal music.)

If you recruited everyone in previous chapters

(Funky music emerges from the cafe and fills the town.) (You are filled with the power of funky town.)

Dark buildings

The largely-identical shops from before the Card Kingdom Darkners are brought to Castle Town. The interaction counter is shared across all 4 interactive buildings.

First time

(It looks like a shop, but the door is locked, and no one's inside...)

Subsequent times

(It's locked.)

Manual

A book on a pillar, located near the SAVE point.

(It's a manual. Read a topic?) If you SPARE or PACIFY enough enemies of the same type, they will be RECRUITed. RECRUITs will appear in your TOWN. Wonder what happens if you get them all...? You can check the area's RECRUIT status at SAVE POINTS. Certain BOSSES are excluded. You will now have access to a STORAGE menu at SAVE POINTS. When you're out of space, ITEMs you find or BUY will be sent to your STORAGE. Feel free to put things inside you might not be able to use yet. Here is a reminder of the controls. Change them in the config menu. [CONFIRM] Confirm and interact with things. [CANCEL] Cancel. Hold to RUN. Shows all text instantly. [MENU] Opens the menu. Hold down to quickly skip textboxes. (There's no time to read books.)

Sign for Top Bakery

Appears alongside the bakery itself after the Card Kingdom Darkners are retrieved.

Mama Miba! Welcome to TOP Bakery! Our special chefs spin baked goods at the TOP of their class!

Music Room

Available for interaction in Chapter 4. Otherwise, the building can be entered without commentary.

(The Music Shop is closed! Everyone's busy this time!)

TV Studio

Available for interaction in Chapter 4 if Tenna didn't survive the events of Chapter 3. Otherwise, the building can be entered without commentary.

(Seems that the building isn't ready to open yet...) (Maybe there wasn't enough staff.)

Rudinn (Town status tracker)

The Rudinn who tells you about how your town is doing. Consistently refers to the town using the player's name, as chosen during the vessel creation sequence.
