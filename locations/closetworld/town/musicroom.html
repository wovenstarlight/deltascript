Play Music

Menu layout

Selecting this option opens a menu which contains text similar to that below on the right:

Page # / # Now Playing # - Song Name Left / Right: Change Pages. Play songs freely.

In the above text:

  • The counter at the top displays the current page number and total page count (e.g. "Page 1 / 3").
  • The "# - Song Name" line (here left static) remains blank until a song is chosen, at which point it begins scrolling from left to right in a radio-like marquee.

On the left of the menu is a numbered list of music tracks, 6 per page. Selecting a track to play causes its name in the list to display in yellow. The track begins playing for as long the player remains in the Music Room, and the track number and name appear under the "Now Playing" heading above.
