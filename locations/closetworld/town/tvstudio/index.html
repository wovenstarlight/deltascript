Zapper

Present at the desk if at least one TV World Darkner has been recruited. Otherwise, the desk is empty and has a note on it.

Zapper Hello, boss. Welcome to TV STUDIO. Zapper Here you can play various MiniGames. Zapper They have no effect on anything, so just enjoy yourself and relax!

Note at empty desk

Present instead of Zapper if all TV World Darkners weren't recruited.

(Now recruiting someone to be at this desk! Yikes!)

Rhythm Game door

Available to interact with only if all TV World Darkners weren't recruited. Otherwise, the door can be walked through without commentary to enter the Rhythm Game hall.

(Currently under construction!) (Would be done if there were more recruits from TV World!)

Mike's door

Chapter 2

When sealed

If Shadowguy, Pippins, and Zapper haven't all been recruited.

(Mystery game! Currently under construction!)

When locked

Initially labelled "MIKE" on both sides; turns into cat-themed doors after fighting "Mike" inside.

"MIKE'S ROOM" It seems to be locked with a code. Enter it?

A four-digit number entry box appears. Dialogue proceeds depending on the combination entered.

Entering the wrong combination

An error buzzer plays as the numbers turn red.

(... wrong combination!)
Entering the right combination

That being 6453 as per the hint note.

A success chime plays as the numbers turn yellow.

(You entered the numbers knowingly.)

The lock disappears, and the door can now be entered without commentary, leading to the MIKE ZONE.

Dialogue ends.

Hint to Mike's door

Located on the wall next to Mike's door at the end of Chapter 4, before the final confrontation.

(The PASSWORD is 6453! Don't forget it...!)
