Poster

Located between the help desk and the song doors.

(RHYTHM GAME! Press the buttons in time with the notes to get a high score!) (Ask PIPPINS and SHADOWGUY for more details!)

Pippins

Present behind the counter with Shadowguy.

Pippins Need any tips? Pippins Alright, listen up. Pippins Keeping in mind this is just for fun... Pippins Time your notes well to get GOLD NOTES. Pippins Well, there might be some lag... just adjust on the fly. Pippins [CONFIRM] and [CANCEL] play the notes... Pippins But you can press [Left] or [Right] to play the notes, too. Or mix-and-match! Pippins Made a mistake? Quit or restart by pressing [MENU]! Pippins Anyway, that's all! Pippins Fair.

Shadowguy

Present behind the counter with Pippins.

(Ask for help?)

Shadowguy makes several saxophone sounds.

Dialogue ends.

Plugboy

Present in front of the counter, beside Shadowguy.

Plugboy Look... I know this guy can't talk. Plugboy But he seems to really really like his job. Plugboy So... let's just let him do it.

Song doors

Each door uses the same narration with different values substituted in.

Interacting with a door

A yellow marquee appears at the top of the screen. The scrolling text reads "! <Song Name> ! High Score (Normal) - <###> ! High Score (Hard) - <###> !", with the name and appropriate numbers substituted in. (See below for a list of available songs.)

The corresponding rank for each score (Z to T) is displayed over the door, with the normal mode rank on the left in yellow and the hard mode rank on the right on orange.

If a song has not been played to completion on a certain mode yet, the corresponding score is listed as 0, and the corresponding rank is given as a simple dash (-).

"<Song Name>"! Play it!?

The doors thud open and the screen fades to black. A countdown from 3 plays, each number appearing with a dramatic sting; after 1, the screen cuts to black with a chime, then fades in rapidly to the Rock Video stage, playing the song on normal mode.

The doors thud open and the screen fades to black. A countdown from 3 plays, each number appearing with a dramatic sting; after 1, the screen cuts to black with a chime, then fades in rapidly to the Rock Video stage, playing the song on hard mode.

Dialogue ends.

Songs playable in the game.

  • It's TV Time! - Chapter 4 onwards
