After retrieving the Card Kingdom Darkners

(There's a hole in the wall...) (There's nothing inside.)

If Jevil's reward from Chapter 1 is pending

That is, if the following conditions are met:

  • Chapter 1 was completed without defeating Jevil
  • This SAVE file was continued into Chapter 2
  • Jevil was then defeated in this SAVE slot

This interaction becomes available only after retrieving the Card Kingdom Darkners. It remains available after returning from Cyber World only if Spamton NEO was defeated.

Something is shining in the hole in the cliff.

(Something is glimmering in the hole.) (Look inside?)
If the applicable equipment inventory is not full

(Armor inventory if Jevil was pacified; weapon inventory if Jevil was defeated violently.)

(You reached inside and found something alongside a strange piece of glass...)
If the inventory doesn't contain a Shadow Crystal
(You got ShadowCrystal.)

A Shadow Crystal is added to the inventory.

If the inventory contains a Shadow Crystal

The number of Crystals increases silently.

Generic text for obtaining the reward plays:

  • armor variant for the Jevilstail, if Jevil was pacified
  • weapon variant for the Devilsknife, if Jevil was defeated violently

The glimmer within the hole disappears.

If the applicable equipment inventory is full

Generic text for being unable to take the reward plays:

  • armor variant for the Jevilstail, if Jevil was pacified
  • weapon variant for the Devilsknife, if Jevil was defeated violently

Dialogue ends.

Chapter 4

Standard

(The rubble on the ground seems to have been dusted and polished.) (Someone around here really cares about the details.)

If Shadow Crystal boss rewards from previous chapters are pending

That is, if the following conditions are met:

  • One or more previous chapters were completed without defeating the Shadow Crystal boss of that chapter
  • This SAVE file was continued into Chapter 4
  • One or more of the Shadow Crystal bosses from previous chapters were then defeated in this SAVE slot

Something is shining in the hole in the cliff.

(Something is glimmering in the hole.) (Look inside?)
If there is room for at least one piece of the pending equipment
(You reached inside and found something alongside a strange piece of glass...)
If the inventory doesn't contain a Shadow Crystal
(You got ShadowCrystal.)

One or more Shadow Crystals are added to the inventory.

If the inventory contains a Shadow Crystal

The number of Crystals increases silently by the pending amount.

Generic text for obtaining the pending reward(s) plays in order of corresponding chapters:

  • armor variant for sparing or pacifying enemies
    • Jevil's Jevilstail
    • Spamton NEO's Dealmaker
  • weapon variant for violently defeating enemies
    • Jevil's Devilsknife
    • Spamton NEO's PuppetScarf
    • The Roaring Knight's Black Knife

Once the relevant inventory is full, the following rewards instead play the equipment-get failure messages (armor or weapon variants, as applicable) and are not added to the inventory. The cliff can be returned to repeatedly to get all pending rewards.

Once all rewards have been taken, the glimmer within the hole disappears.

If all applicable equipment inventories are full

Such that not a single reward can be taken.

Generic text for failure to take only the FIRST pending reward plays:

  • armor variant for the armors listed above
  • weapon variant for the weapons listed above

Dialogue ends.

Immediately after taking all pending Shadow Crystal boss rewards

That is, before leaving and reentering the area.

Treasure chest

Chapter 2

Appears after returning from Cyber World if the following conditions are met:

  • Spamton NEO was defeated, regardless of method and route
  • The applicable equipment inventory was full at the time of the victory
  • (Normal route only) The reward was not taken from the treasure chest in the mansion basement

Uses generic text for treasure chests:

  • armor variant for the Dealmaker if Spamton NEO was spared
  • weapon variant for the PuppetScarf if Spamton NEO was defeated
