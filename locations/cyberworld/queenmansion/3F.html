Entrance hall of 3F

The hall first entered after coming up from 2F.

Bookcase (Shortcut to 1F)

Exclusive to the normal route. (The shortcut is present on the Weird Route, but without the bookcase.)

(There's a book here about preventing backtracking.)

Bookcase (Shortcut to 4F)

Exclusive to the normal route. (The shortcut is present on the Weird Route, but without the bookcase.)

(There's a book here about secret passageway construction.)

SAVE point (start of 3F)

Exclusive to the normal route. Blocks the entrance to 3F after talking to Berdly; disappears after crossing the acid lake. Describes the location as "Queen's Mansion - 3F".

Susie has gone into a book hole with Berdly. You are filled with the power of book holes.

Toilet

Located inside a bathroom to the top right

Icon Man

Present outside the door to the bathroom after the party has checked the statue inside.

Icon Man When are they gonna be done in there?

Bathroom

Contains only a single toilet which is blocked by the golden Berdly statue, though without the Queen attachment.

First time

(The statue of Berdly is wedged in the toilet.) (You glanced back at the bathroom door sign.) (The sign has a silhouette of a Berdly statue.) (Everything checks out.)

Subsequent times

(No problem here.)

Projection

A small room to the west. The interactions below are exclusive to the normal route; on the Weird Route, this room contains only a hostile Werewerewire.

Video

Played on the far wall; depicts the Knight creating a Dark Fountain.

(It's a video of someone creating a strange pillar of smoke.) (There's so much smoke, it's hard to tell who it is.)

Yellow Swatchling

First time

Swatchling This is today's dinner entertainment. Swatchling I've heard it's called "The Birth of a Fountain." Swatchling What an elegant name.

Subsequent times

Swatchling How elegantly quixotic!

Red Swatchling

First time

Swatchling The QUEEN has been very interested in this video recently. Swatchling Where the KNIGHT creates the Fountain by its own hand... Swatchling Well, it's that and Cyber City's Funniest Home Videos.

Subsequent times

Swatchling We are often forced to star in those videos.

Orange Swatchling

First time

Swatchling The KNIGHT created the FOUNTAIN, and this WORLD. Swatchling It might be even more powerful than Queen. Swatchling ... for now.

Subsequent times

Swatchling ... for now.

Acid river

Exclusive to the normal route; all objects/characters listed below are absent on the Weird Route.

Swan boat lever

Located in the room where the acid river starts, the only interactive object there.

Before first crossing the lake

First time

The lever releases a swan boat that floats to a stop in front of Kris and Ralsei.

I suppose... this... works. Haha.
Subsequent times
(The lever is stuck.)

After first crossing the lake

First time
(Pulling this lever releases a swan boat.) (You thought about releasing a ton of swans to clog the river...)
Subsequent times
(Aww, are they reminiscing about our ride...?)

Giant hand

Blocking the way through the acid river. (For the area past the nearby door, see the house puzzles section below.)

A giant hand... Sure would be nice to be pet by something like that!

Swan boat

After being stopped by the giant hand

(References Queen's dialogue from outside the mansion.)

(Looks like a certain bird is having a ride on the acid river.)

After first crossing the acid river

(Looks like it's completed its migration.)

Green Swatchling

Present at the end of the acid river only if you took a photo with Ralsei.

Swatchling I was fishing for lost pottery in the acid when I found this photo. Swatchling Please take a look.

If you hugged Ralsei

U-umm, it sure is a nice photo... Susie, do you want it for your room? The hell would I want this for!?

If you made a peace sign

Kris, you look nice in this one!

If you made a rude gesture

Hahaha!! That's awesome, Kris!

House puzzle area

Exclusive to the normal route. Located partway through the acid tunnel ride.

Castle

A miniature located on the northwest island of the central area.

Before fighting the nearby Werewire

(This castle looks like it has worn many seiges...)

After fighting the nearby Werewire

(It seems that peace has returned to the castle...)

Treasure chest

Left behind after the Werewires are defeated.

Uses generic text for a treasure chest containing the item Revive Dust.

Red Swatchling

Located in the eastern area.

Initial

After the Swatchling has been released from the northeast island.

Swatchling Thank you for your help. Swatchling I had gone up there to do a little house cleaning and got stuck.

After the vase has been released

By triggering the dummy house on the northern island.

Swatchling ...

Immediately after breaking the vase

Since it's on the pathway leading out of this area, you can't avoid it.

Swatchling turns hostile and approaches Kris and Ralsei with a broom, but stops and loses its hostile aura upon reaching them. They turn to face Swatchling.

Swatchling ... Swatchling I suppose we'll just have to let it go this once.

Swatchling puts away the broom and walks away down the path past Kris and Ralsei, who turn to watch it pass. Once it's gone, control is returned to the player.

Sign for mouse puzzles

Located in the northern area beside the mouse puzzle.

(Dear guests, please note our mice WILL turn at every crossing.) (Our vermin have been specially hand-picked for higher turning. Enjoy.)

Mice's treasure chests

Narration for when the mice are directed into the treasure chests. (Sending the mice to the treasure chests affects Mousemillian's dialogue on 2F.)

Top chest

(The mice found $20.) (You didn't get any.)

Bottom chest

(The mice found $1.) (You didn't get any.)

Solving the mouse puzzle

Narration for when both miniature houses have been activated by the mice.

A thud sounds.

(Sounds like the bridge was raised.)

Giant hand lever

Located in the northwest area at the end of the path, near a giant hand.

First time

A second giant hand — presumably the one blocking the swan boat's way — rises out of the acid, and the two hands high-five each other with a loud smack.

(Looks like the hand is out of the way now...)

Subsequent times

(Pulling the lever back would undo the high-five.) (You can't undo a high-five.)

SAVE point (end of 3F)

Exclusive to the normal route. Located immediately before the stairs to 4F. Describes the location as "Queen's Mansion - Acid Tunnel".

Everyone is together, but the wind blows coldly... You are filled with a certain power.
