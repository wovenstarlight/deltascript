General flavor text

Initial encounter

A mysterious festival begins.

Neutral

Ribbons flutter in soft chaos.
Petals fall, and dance.
Playful knives juggle in the air.

3% chance:

Smells like hydrangea.

After using specific ACTs

Check

The enemy analyzes the stats of random things.

Spin

The enemy revolves. The world revolves, too.

Dance

The enemy dances, dances, falls, and dances.

Care

The enemy makes a patient's wristband out of a ribbon.

Coolpose

The enemy invents several million new poses.

Flatter

The enemy praises herself under her breath for every action.

Low HP

The enemy seems damaged, but having fun.

Bored

Uses a counter that is added to every time any ACT is used more than once.

First repetition

... but the enemy was not amused.

Followed by the corresponding dialogue.

Second repetition

... but the enemy seemed bored.

Followed by the corresponding dialogue.

Third repetition

... but the enemy seemed very bored.

Followed by the corresponding dialogue.

General dialogue

Neutral

Aqua Are we playing a game?
Aqua Is the world revolving, like they say?
Aqua Uee hee hee! It's so much fun!
Aqua Let's play knife throwing game!

Aqua Uuu, what is this!? "Pain?" How funny!! Aqua Did you know? You should try some too!

End of fight

Plays immediately before Aqua's attack phase as soon as either of the below conditions are met, following any ACT-specific dialogue for that turn.

If Aqua has gained 100% Mercy or grown too bored

Aqua Here, I'll do something, and you copy! Aqua My magic, my Omega, can you follow!?

Aqua uses her Omega Knife attack. Once it ends, she speaks again, beginning to laugh:

Aqua Uee hee hee!! Now, your turn, your turn!! Aqua Magic, magic, Omega, magic!

She continues laughing for a moment. Then her smile drops, the music cutting short.

Aqua ... what? Aqua You can't do it!? Aqua Uuu, how boring... Aqua No more battle!

The encounter ends.

If Aqua is at 20% HP or below

Aqua Uuu, this is fun, fun!!! Seth Wh-what are you doing!? Aqua Playing a game! Do you want to play, too!? Seth What!? NO!! If you keep fighting, you'll end up... Aqua Uuu? What, what? End up what? Is it another type of game...? Seth N-No, I'm just worried that...

Aqua begins laughing.

Aqua Don't worry, you can play too!! Seth That's not what I'm saying!!! Just give up!! I'm going to retreat, too!

Aqua stops laughing and puts her hands on her hips.

Aqua Uuu, okay...

The encounter ends.
