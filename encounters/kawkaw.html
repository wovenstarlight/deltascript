Description

Leave or Love

Flavor text

Instructions

[CONFIRM] repeatedly to Call Over! [MENU] repeatedly to Shoo!

A timer lasting around 7 seconds appears.

Pressing [CONFIRM] makes all party members beckon Kawkaw closer; it happily scoots forward, approaching the party members.

Pressing [MENU] makes all party members wave Kawkaw away; a large teardrop hangs from its distraught eye, both eye and tear growing bigger with every Shoo.

Outcome

Successfully called over

Kawkaw gains 100% Mercy. It ducks into the ground and pops up in front of each party member, licking them, before ducking down and sprouting back up in its original spot.

Standard
It's overjoyed! Sticky saliva made SOUL slower!
If Shi is present
It's overjoyed! Shi was not! Sticky saliva made SOUL slower!

Shi becomes Tired.

The SOUL moves slower during the following attack phase, indicated by a wobbly jelly-like outline.

Successfully shooed
It was utterly rejected!

Kawkaw flees the battle.

If Shi is present
Shi seemed happy...?

Shi gains 60% Mercy.

Both shooed and called

If both inputs are pressed, but neither one reaches the margin of success.

Everyone got mixed messages!

All enemies become Tired, and Kawkaw gains some Mercy proportional to the number of times "Shoo" or "Call" was pressed, whichever is greater.

Ran out of time

Kawkaw returns to its original spot and expression, with a small question mark bubble appearing over its head.

Ran out of time!

All enemies become Tired.
