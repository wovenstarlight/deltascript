Description

Flavor text

Instructions

Press the directions to CUT on the dotted line!

The party members wear doctor/nurse outfits, each holding out a pair of scissors. In the center of the screen, a dotted outline appears with a large pair of scissors situated somewhere on it. Inputting directions, as if moving the SOUL, will move the scissors to "cut" along the dotted line. A timer appears at the bottom and begins counting down once the player starts cutting. The timer lasts a little over 15 seconds or longer depending on the chosen outline.

The ACT ends when the player's cutting line meets itself, and the party members switch to holding up paper crafts (a snowflake for Kris, an airplane for Susie, and a string of figures for Ralsei). Depending on the accuracy of the cut, Sheary can gain up to 100% Mercy.

Outcome

94% Mercy or higher
Amazing cutting skills!!!
Between 80% and 93% Mercy
Great job!
Between 40% and 79% Mercy
Not bad!
39% Mercy or lower
Arts and crafts failure!
If the scissors go outside the screen bounds

A audience gasp sounds and the ACT ends early, with Sheary not gaining any Mercy.

Truancy. Failure.
