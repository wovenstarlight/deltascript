Flavor text

Instructions

Press with the right timing to INTIMIDATE!

A meter appears with a Kris-shaped hole at the center, with text below reading "HIT IT!". A silhouette of Kris slides across the meter; [CONFIRM] must be pressed while the silhouette is at the center.

Outcome

Perfectly-centered hit

All party members strike a T-pose.

You engaged in a perfect pose! It was highly effective!

Shi gains 100% Mercy.

Off-center hit

All party members strike a T-pose.

You engaged in a superior pose! It was very effective!

Shi gains between 50% and 70% Mercy depending on the precision of the hit.

Very off-center hit / Miss

An error buzzer plays. All party members strike varying letter-like poses—Y for Kris, M for Susie, and C for Ralsei.

The pose was mistaken! It wasn't very effective!

Shi gains 50% Mercy.

All other enemies gain 10% Mercy, regardless of your success.
