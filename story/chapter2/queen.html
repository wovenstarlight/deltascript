Encountering Queen

As the party walks out into a wider section of the roof, the howling of the wind fades to silence as Susie notices something up ahead, losing her smile and stopping the party.

There she is!

The camera pans over as the party hurries forward, fanning out to face Queen in her rocket-powered chair. She's facing away from the party and doesn't react to their presence; Susie steps forward after a pause.

Queen!!!! Stop right there!!!

Queen's chair turns around as she finally notices them.

Ah My Sweet Idiot Children You Are Just In Time To Witness My World Domination With Noelle In My Possession It Is Only A Matter Of Time Before Her Will Turns And She Unleashes Her Power Blanketing The World In --

Susie points at Queen with a smile.

Too late, dumbass! Noelle's with Berdly! Oh Then Who This

Queen laughs as Berdly drops down between her and the party members, a control plug on his face. The party members pull backwards, Susie and Ralsei looking shocked. Ominous music begins playing.

B... Berdly!? But he's been...

Queen stops laughing and raises her glass of acid in a mock-toast. As she speaks, Berdly is pulled back to hang alongside her.

Intercepted My Control Plugs Are Now Suitable For Teenagers Now The Whole Family Can Enjoy Eternal Servitude

Queen lowers her glass and laughs silently.

(Sort Of Regal Mostly Cruel Laughter) Wait, then... where's Noelle!?? (Laughter Becomes More Cruel) Oh I Wonder (I Actually Know The Answer)

Queen stops laughing as the party members ready their weapons.

Enough already! Oh Dear I Do Sincerely Quite Like You Two It Will Be A Shame To Force You To Serve Me An Enjoyable Shame

The music cuts to silence and Queen laughs for a moment before the battle begins.

After the fight

If Berdly was brought to 100% Mercy, and was spared/pacified in both previous fights

The screen flashes white as the encounter ends; when the brightness fades, Berdly is standing unhindered at center stage, carrying his halberd and ready to fight. Behind him, the party looks on seriously. Queen's chair is gone and she's clutching her head in shock.

Queen...! You may have mastered the number three... But can you stand up against FOUR of us!?

Queen turns aside, away from the player.

Running Simulation For: The Number Four

Queen turns to Berdly and the others, no longer smiling.

... Hmm

Queen turns aside, facing the player, smile regained.

You Are Definitely Going To Kick: My Ass Unless

Queen points dramatically towards the party… or at something behind them.

Oh No What That

The party and Berdly all turn to look behind themselves. Queen seizes the moment to run offscreen with a hoot of laughter. Once she's gone, the party turns back around; Berdly is stunned by her disappearance.

W-wait! Wait one nanosecond! You... you cowardette! You... you...

Berdly drops to his knees, shocking Susie and Ralsei. They hurry over to him.

Alas... B-Berdly!? Haha, just now, was nothing more than simple bravado... The truth is, that wire... Already absorbed all of my succulent energy juices.

Dialogue proceeds to parting ways with Berdly.

If Queen('s chair) was brought to 0% HP, or Berdly was violently defeated in one or both previous fights

As the encounter ends, the party members walk forward. Queen, still in her chair, looks shocked.

Oh Dear It Seems Someone Sipped Up All Of My Shield Power In other words...

The party members stride forwards, and Susie points at Queen with a smile.

Nothing's stopping us from beating you up now, huh?

Queen smiles.

Well Not Nothing

Queen lifts her glass of acid as if in a toast, and Berdly is lifted on his control wire. He drops down in front of the party, startling them backwards. Queen lowers her glass and continues speaking.

Mr. Birdy Here Still Has Maximum HP So Unless You Want Your Dear Friend To Get Hurt I Recommend

Queen raises her glass for a moment, and Berdly creates several bursts of tornadoes as she does so.

Letting Him Hurt You Instead

Queen laughs, but freezes when Berdly suddenly turns towards her, one hand coming up to the edge of the control wire's plug.

Berdly You... won't... control... me...

Queen looks shocked.

What What Are You Doing

Berdly begins pulling at the plug; he and the wire shake, smoke and arcs of electricity coming from where the plug rests on his face.

Berdly Taking... off... this... plug!

The plug and Berdly's arm begin flashing yellow.

Stop-- Stop That At Once Your Arm Will--

An explosion goes off, a burst of light coming from the plug as Berdly pulls it off his face. Queen is knocked offscreen in her chair from the force of the blast, shouting as she goes:

STOP!!!

The brightness fades to show Berdly standing upright and facing the party with a serious expression, the control wire gone. The party members look back at him, similarly serious. A moment later, though, Berdly falls to his knees, startling Susie and Ralsei. The two of them hurry over to Berdly.

Berdly... what the hell, man!? Your arm is... That was stupid...! Yes... It was, wasn't it...? IT'S NOT PRAISE!! Kris. Susie. I can't do much with a fried wing... Please... promise. You'll save Noelle. We... we promise!

Dialogue proceeds to parting ways with Berdly.

Parting ways with Berdly

Berdly falls back, lying supine on the ground; Susie and Ralsei are shocked, though Ralsei straightens up after a moment.

Berdly!! Hey!! Hey, Berdly!! Are you OK!? Everything... getting dark... (Only...)

Berdly puckers his lips (beak?) as if to kiss something.

(Only a kiss from a beautiful gamer girl can save me now...)

Susie walks over and kneels beside Berdly.

Berdly...

The screen begins fading to pink with a chime… but the pink abruptly disappears when Susie speaks, Berdly's eyes going wide and mouth going slack in surprise.

What? Like, get you're, like, losing consciousness and stuff, but, Could you speak up?

Berdly pushes himself back up and onto his knees. Susie stands up as he does so.

Umm, aha, s-sorry, suddenly I... I... I'm feeling a bit better! Hey, that's a relief!

Susie turns to the right and takes a few steps away, then stops and turns to face Berdly.

Well, uh, gotta hurry and save Noelle, so... later.

She walks offscreen.

Ahh, dear Susan. So enigmatic in her ways. But I can read her heart, Kris. Like a health meter. And it's beeping... for love. Haha! Well enough tales, Kris. Good luck. May the smarts... NOT be with you.

Ralsei hurries over to stand beside Kris on the other side from Berdly; the camera follows him, and Kris turns to face him when he stops behind them.

Okay.

Ralsei regains his smile as control is returned to the player, who may speak to Berdly and stop by the nearby newly-spawned SAVE point before going after Queen.
