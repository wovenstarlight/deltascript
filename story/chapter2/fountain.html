Normal route

The screen is black. Dramatic music plays as the Fountain fades into view; Kris and Susie approach it slowly.

Susie Kris...

Susie turns to Kris. A pause.

Susie It looks like we're here again, huh... Susie The Roaring Knight... Susie Whatever they are, they're making these fountains... Susie ...

Susie turns to the Fountain. A pause.

Susie But... is that really a bad thing...? Susie Since they started showing up, Susie Everything's gotten a lot more interesting, hasn't it...? Susie ... Susie Well, we don't have to think about that now... Susie Kris, it's your turn.

Kris walks up to the Fountain.

(It was as if your very SOUL was glowing...)

The music cuts off as the SOUL flashes into view over Kris. A short riff plays as it floats forward into the center of the Fountain, illuminating it from inside as the SOUL glows bright enough to turn the screen white. The screen then fades into black as everyone returns to the Light World.

Weird Route

Reaching the Fountain

After a Weird Route-exclusive area including a SAVE point and a vending machine selling CD Bagels, Kris reaches a large doorway leading to the Fountain.

Upon entering, the screen fades to black. The Fountain then fades into view; Kris approaches it slowly and stands in front of it in silence for a moment. Silent narration starts:

(It was as if your very SOUL was glowing...)

A long pause.

Spamton NEO NOT!!!

A silhouetted figure drops down from above the Fountain, hanging from green strings. Kris is thrown to the left, falling to their knees. The figure lifts up and offscreen, then drops back down on the right side of the room. Kris stands and faces the figure, who fades into view, revealing himself as Spamton NEO; a slow, ominous version of Spamton's theme plays as he speaks.

Spamton NEO LET ME SAY Spamton NEO LET ME SAY [Thanks ] Spamton NEO THANKS TO YOUR [Total Jackass stunts] I HAVE [Becomed] NEO. Spamton NEO AND NOW IT'S MY [Mansion]! MY [City]! MY [World]!

Spamton NEO turns his head to the side facing the player and his swaying limbs freeze in place; his whole body trembles.

Spamton NEO SO WHY ARE YOU [Stealing] THE [Fountain]!? Spamton NEO TO [$!$!] ME OVER RIGHT AT THE [Good part]!? WHAT ARE YOU, A [Gameshow Host]!?

He looks forward again, limbs swaying freely once more.

Spamton NEO AH, KID, FORGET IT. I'M AN [HonestMan]. Spamton NEO I'LL LET YOU [Pay] YOUR WAY OUT OF THIS ONE!!

He turns his head to the side.

Spamton NEO [Pay]...

A slight pause. Then Spamton NEO looks at Kris and points his right arm at them, the end transformed into a cannon.

Spamton NEO WITH YOUR [Rapidly-Shrinking] LIFE!!!

Spamton NEO begins laughing, head jerking from position to position. The ominous music rapidly speeds up until it and Spamton NEO's laugh finally cut off, and the fight begins.

After the fight

After Noelle freezes Spamton NEO, the screen remains black and silent for a moment. The SOUL then flashes into view towards the bottom of the screen. In the continued silence, it floats up and stops where the Fountain was last visible. After a moment, it flashes again and glows bright enough to turn the screen white. The screen then fades into black as everyone returns to the Light World.
