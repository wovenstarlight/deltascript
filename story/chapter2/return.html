Waking up - Normal route

The darkness flashes white, then fades to reveal the computer lab in the Hometown library. Kris and Susie stand near the entrance, while Noelle and Berdly are both sitting at a desk with their heads down on the table, pillowed by their arms. After a moment, Berdly lifts his head, squinting blearily.

H... huh...? What... what happened?

Noelle also lifts her head, smiling with her eyes similarly half-closed.

Oh... it WAS a dream, wasn't it... Huh... A dream? That was all... A DREAM? ... ... Of... of course!! That MUST have been a dream!! Such feelings... such thoughts... Oh, sweet lady of justice!! To think, I would ever think Susie would be...

A pause. Berdly and Noelle jolt, looking at Susie and Kris in shock.

SUSIE!? S... Susie!? Wh... What are you doing here!?

Susie turns to Kris.

Uhhhh, ... you invited us to study. Remember?

She turns back to the others; they look calmer, wearing their usual smiles.

O-oh, right, I, err-- I--

Berdly turns to Noelle, who looks at him as well.

What!? Studying with THEM?? Noelle!! Heavens to yikes!! They're... they're...

Berdly looks at Susie and Kris.

...

He looks at Noelle.

... Well, I guess we could... TRY it.

A phone rings. After a slight pause, Berdly jolts in shock.

H... Huh?? My... my alarm?? Ahh, I'm going to be late for my shift with Ms. Boom!!

Noelle jolts, similarly shocked.

Oh no, how is it that late already!?

If Berdly's arm was injured in the fight against Queen

Berdly shifts towards the edge of the desk.

Noelle looks at Berdly as he speaks.

Well, adieu, everyone. I must gather my booklongings.

If Berdly wasn't injured in the fight against Queen

Berdly grabs his books and stands up, backing away from the desk.

Ahh, how I love the texture of freshly-picked-up books!

Noelle also picks up her books and stands; she and Berdly face each other.

Now, let us hasten, my dear Noelle!

Noelle leads Berdly towards the door to the computer lab.

If Berdly's arm was injured in the fight against Queen

Berdly squints down at his right arm, confused by something. Using his left hand, he lifts his right arm onto the desk, looking concerned.

H... huh? That's funny, my right arm... won't move. O-oh...? It must have fallen asleep.

Noelle slides over to Berdly's side of the desk and picks up both their books, standing and stepping back from the desk.

Here. I'll carry your books for you, Berdly.

Berdly stands as well, arm hanging limp at his side.

Ah, Noelle... You're... always helping me. Thank you.

Noelle leads Berdly towards the door to the computer lab; his right arm continues hanging limp as he walks, unlike his unscathed left arm swinging normally.

As Noelle and Berdly walk, Susie and Kris turn to watch; the two of them stop in front of Susie and Kris respectively.

... Good day, Kris. ... Susie.

Berdly turns around and leaves the computer lab.

Bye Kris! And... umm... bye, Susie!

Kris and Susie both turn to the left, looking towards Noelle.

Umm... see ya.

They turn to look further into the computer lab. Noelle begins to walk towards the door, but stops after a moment and turns around, slowly tipping her head to the side as though trying to get a better look at something; Susie stands directly in her line of sight. A pause. Susie whips around, looking thrown off; Kris turns to face Noelle as well. With a gasp, Susie charges a step forward, looking furious.

WHAT!? WHAT!? Are you looking for something!?

Noelle turns and flees.

N-nothing!! B-bye!!

The door closes behind Noelle. Susie turns around and walks back up beside Kris more calmly, looking at the computer lab while Kris is still turned towards the door. A pause. Susie turns to Kris.

The heck is her problem, anyway? Way to treat someone who just saved the WORLD.

Scene proceeds to Susie's realization.

Waking up - Weird Route

The darkness flashes white, then fades to reveal the computer lab in the Hometown library. Kris and Susie stand near the entrance, while Noelle and Berdly are both sitting at a desk with their heads down on the table, pillowed by their arms. After a moment, a phone rings twice; Noelle lifts her head at the first ring, eyes still half-closed and smiling.

H-huh... Berdly's... alarm? A dream...? It was really just a...

Noelle jolts, looking at Susie and Kris in shock. A pause.

Susie!? Susie! What are you doing here? Uhh... You invited us to study. Remember?

Noelle calms down, smiling as usual.

Oh, right! I did, didn't I! Haha! ... uhh, you're in a good mood. Did you, uh, have a good dream? It was a nightmare. Oh. I'm... just happy I woke up. ... The... end was nice, though. What happened?

Noelle jolts, looking panicked.

HAHA, w-well, umm ---

She looks towards Berdly.

HAHA HEY, Berdly time to get up and go!

A long pause. Noelle's smile drops.

... Berdly?

A long pause. Abruptly, Noelle slides over to Berdly's side of the desk and picks up both their books, standing and rapidly backing away from the desk.

Gosh, you've been studying too much, Berdly. Honestly, you deserve a little rest, y'know? Sweet dreams!

Noelle walks around the desk towards the entrance of the lab, Kris and Susie turning to watch. Noelle stops in front of Susie and tilts her head.

Wh... what are you doing? You don't have a tail, do you, Susie? H-huh!? N-no way, of course not! Really? That's great!

Noelle turns around and walks out of the computer lab, still smiling. A slight pause.

...That was weird, Kris. Somehow, it doesn't feel like we just saved the world...

Scene proceeds to Susie's realization.

Susie's realization

Susie jolts, reeling in shock. A pause. She grabs Kris by the shoulders and shakes them rapidly.

KRIS!! Hey, wait a sec, Kris!! We... we just actually saved the world, didn't we!? Damn, we really are heroes! And no one even knows!

She stops shaking Kris and turns to the lab door.

... guess it's better that way though, right?

Susie turns to Kris.

People'd freak out if they knew the world's in danger.

Susie turns to the lab, scratching her head.

... though, kinda sucks Noelle has to forget, too... ...

She turns to Kris.

Whatever. Let's get out of here.

Susie walks out of the lab; Kris turns to the door, watching her go. The door is heard closing behind Susie.

Control is returned to the player, who can explore the computer lab.

On the Weird Route only

Berdly remains facedown on the desk and can be checked on, giving the following narration:

(He doesn't seem to be awake.)

At any point, the player may direct Kris to leave the computer lab, at which point the screen fades slowly to black.

(Before you left, you gathered up everything in the room...)

After a pause, the screen brightens to show Kris on the lower floor of the library, just outside the computer lab doors. The room is empty.

Leaving the library

Kris walks slowly out of the library and stops on the sidewalk. Susie is leaning against the nearby wall post, arms folded.

... Man, it got late, didn't it...?

A pause. Kris turns to the left, facing Susie.

... guess you should go home, huh?

A pause. Susie straightens up and turns to Kris, smiling.

Alright, you don't have to say it.

She walks up in front of them.

Don't wanna walk home by yourself, huh?

She turns aside, facing away from the player.

Well, if you're gonna MAKE me, I guess...

She moves to stand behind Kris.

Let's go.

Control is returned to the player, who may explore Hometown to their satisfaction, after which they may return to the Dreemurr residence.
