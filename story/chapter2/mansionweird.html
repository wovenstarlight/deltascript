Entering the mansion - 1F

Spotting Rouxls Kaard

Upon entering the mansion and advancing into the foyer, Susie and Ralsei break away from the party to stand in front of the statue of Rouxls.

Huh...? Isn't this...

Susie turns to Ralsei. A slight pause.

Uhh, that guy from before?

She turns to look at Rouxls again.

Why is there a statue of him? It's... not a statue, Susie. Huh? You see, each Dark Fountain creates a different "world." A "world" whose Darkners reflect the will of its fountain. But, though those Darkners can exist in their own worlds... They might not "belong" if they go to another one.

A pause.

Huh...

Susie turns to Ralsei.

So if we just bring him back to the castle town... ...

She turns back to Rouxls. A slight pause.

Do we, uh, have to do that? Umm, yes. Whatever.

Susie and Ralsei get back in line behind Kris, and control is returned to the player. The statue continues to block the way to the Color Cafe and cannot be moved, preventing access to Swatch's shop on the Weird Route altogether.

Heading up through the mansion - 2F and 3F

The hallway portraits on floor 2F have been defaced, bearing Spamton's visage instead of Queen's, some captured mid-laugh. Mauswheel roams the corridors and can optionally be fought. A narration-less forcefield blocks the way to Dinner Party Section A, entirely cutting off access to the east wing of 1F. The kitchen door to the west is closed, with interaction only giving the following text:

(Closed.)

The east dining hall door is open and can be entered; inside is a maze of Pipis which trigger encounters when touched. Past the maze and through the long hall at the far end, another forcefield blocks the way to the supreme table. The only available path is to the west. Along this path await more Pipis (mounted on wheels and patrolling outside the locked platter room) as well as a hostile Tasque Manager.

Beyond Tasque Manager is the teacup ride up to 3F; notably, this ride lacks the teacup globules that drench the party on the normal route. 3F itself is entirely devoid of any interactive objects or entities. The only event on this floor is an optional encounter with a hostile Werewerewire to the west. At any time, the player may take the open-by-default shortcut leading upstairs.

Discovering Noelle's room - 4F

The path to the left is blocked by another silent forcefield. The party must proceed right; as they pass a closed door, Susie stops and turns to it, seeming to realize something.

H... huh?

Kris and Ralsei also turn to the door as Susie hurries up to it. After a pause, she turns back to them.

Hey, it says this is Noelle's room! Kris, let's go in and save her! And uh, y'know, teach her not to get in our way.

A pause. Susie waits, but seems to receive no response.

... Kris?

She turns to the door.

Fine, I'll do it myself!

She opens and walks through the door, slamming it shut behind her. A long pause. Then Ralsei turns to Kris.

Kris... Don't you wonder... how they're doing in there?

He faces the door again. Another long pause. Once again, Ralsei turns to Kris:

Umm, I said, don't you wonder... ... how Noelle and Susie are doing?

He faces the door again. Another long pause. Once again, Ralsei turns to Kris:

Y-you're right, Kris, it has only been 30 seconds... (I'll wait a minute first...)

Ralsei turns to the door. A long moment passes; Ralsei transitions to dozing off at some point, while Kris remains standing and staring at the door. After a moment, Susie slams open the door, shocking Ralsei awake; she charges out, slamming the door shut behind herself, and stops in front of Kris with a smile.

Alright let's go!

Ralsei jolts.

H-huh? What!? W-wait -- wait!? Wait, we were supposed to -- Hey, let's go!

Ralsei turns to face Susie, jogging agitatedly in place.

W-wait! Wait, Susie!

Susie turns to him.

What, um, happened in there?

Ralsei goes still when Susie speaks:

What do you mean what happened? U-umm... in there, with Noelle! Nothin. N... Nothing? Nothing at all? Just told her everything is a dream. Now she's feeling better. ... anything else? No and let's go.

Susie turns around to face Kris.

Psst... I'll tell you later, Kris. HEY! (Oh well, as long as Susie's happy... right?)

Control is returned to the player. The door to Noelle's room cannot be interacted with, and the party must proceed eastwards past the electric plug obstacles and a large platform overlooking the fountain. The walkway on the other side leads to Queen.
